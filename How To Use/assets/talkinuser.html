Introduction

Discover global hosts, chat and call freely, and share your moments.Customize your profile, and connect in your own language.

Login Details

Register

  • Tap “Get Register” if you're a new user and don't have an account yet.
  • You'll be taken to the registration form, where you can:

Email Login

  • Tap on the email login fields to enter your registered email and password. After entering details, tap Continue to log in securely.

Mobile Login

  • Select Mobile Login to log in with your phone number.
  • Enter your number and tap Get OTP.
  • You'll receive a One-Time Password (OTP) to verify your number and access your account.

Forgot Password

  • Tap "Forgot Password ?" to reset your account password. Enter your email or mobile number to receive a recovery link or OTP.

Google Login & Quick Log In

  • Tap Google Login to sign in instantly with your Google account.
  • No password needed — fast and secure authentication via your Google credentials.
  • Tap Quick Log In for instant access using previously saved credentials.
  • No need to fill in anything — one tap and you're in (only works if you've used the app before on the same device).

Home

Home

  • Main tagline: "Talk to your Favorite Listener and feel lighter and brighter!"
  • Options: Find listeners and view top listeners.
  • Navigation buttons at the bottom: "View Profile," "Talk Now,".
  • Quick Reconnect: Tap any recent search (e.g., "Hello bro, 25") to view their profile or start a new chat.
  • Save Time: Avoid re-searching – your frequently contacted listeners appear here for easy access.

View profile

  • Check Availability: If "Offline," wait or browse others; if online, tap "Chat Now" or "Talk Now" to connect.
  • Review Profile: Note languages (English/Spanish/French), topics (Daily Challenges), and ratings (17.0) before starting.

Talk now

  • Start a Call: Select "Audio Call" or "Video Call" to connect instantly.

Notification

  • View Alerts: See all missed calls and unread messages (text/audio/images) with timestamps.
  • Manage Notifications:Use "Clear" (top-right) to remove notifications.

Listener Details

  • Filter by Language: Tap "APP Language" to select preferred languages (e.g., Japanese/English).
  • Filter by Topic: Choose "Talk About" (e.g., Life Planning/Stress & Balance) to find specialized listeners.
  • Apply & Connect: Hit "Submit" to see matching listeners, then tap "Talk Now" to start.

Random Call

  • Select Call Type: Choose between Audio Call or Video Call.
  • Start Matching: Tap "Random Match" to connect with a listener instantly.
  • Connect or Chat: Once matched, use the Call or Chat buttons to begin your conversation.

Chat Details

  • Access Chats: Tap any user (e.g., Hello bro) from the chat list to open conversation history.
  • Send Messages: Type in the text box, or use Audio Call/Image options for multimedia communication.

Calling history

  • Review Activity: View all missed/received calls History with timestamps.
  • Quick Reconnect: Tap "Talk Now" next to any entry to instantly call back the listener.

Profile Details

View & Edit Profile

  • Tap "Edit Profile" to update your details (name, email, photo, etc.) → Tap "SAVE PROFILE" to confirm changes.

Wallet

  • Check Balance & Buy Coins:View your Current Coin Balance and tap Any coin plan to purchase plans.
  • Payment Methods:Select Any payment method to complete transactions (History tracks all purchases).
  • View History:View Coin History shows call durations/coin deductions and top-up records.
  • Filter by date: Select specific periods under "Select Date".

Help Center

  • Browse FAQs: Tap any question (e.g., "How do I become a User?") to view instant answers.
  • Contact Support: Select "Have an Issue?" to email the support team for unresolved queries.

Settings

  • Customize Preferences: Toggle options like APP Language or Notifications for personalized experience.
  • Account Actions: Select Logout or Delete Account (confirm carefully as deletion is permanent).

Language

  • Select Preferred Language: Choose from the list (e.g., English, Hindi, etc.) to change app language instantly.
  • Auto-Apply: Selected language updates all app text/settings immediately.
